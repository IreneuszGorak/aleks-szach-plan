# Nakrycie

## World

Bottle-green awning on a cool gray-green paper. The only warm object is a thermal ticket.

## Palette

- Awning `#12392C`, foam text `#F4F7F4`, mist `#D5E3DA`
- Paper `#E7EEE8`, ink `#14241C`
- Ticket `#F4EFE2`, ticket ink `#231E16`, soft `#6A5D4C`
- Warning `#7A3030`

## Type

- Familjen Grotesk for the interface
- Fragment Mono for prices, totals, and the LIDL line

## Signature

A receipt with a scalloped top. Catalogue lines use the shelf price times whole packs. Other lines say „zużycie” and the rate per 100 g. The total is the sum of those lines.

## Layout

Landing: headline and button on the left, ticket on the right, one green field. Plan: one question at a time. Result: the week on the left, the ticket sticky on the right.

## Photos

Meal and product images come only from URLs in the data. Nothing is drawn or invented. There are no shipped raster assets.
